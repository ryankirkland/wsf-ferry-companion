Honest cost

What this direction demands

  • Two or three line icons and a marker system; everything else is geometry, tokens, and typography. Near-zero asset maintenance.
  • Scales perfectly from watch to wall; night mode is a token swap, not a repaint.
  • The risk is blandness: without disciplined motion (wakes, arrival ripples) and warm copy, this collapses into another clean dashboard. The whimsy budget moves entirely into interaction and voice.
  • Payoff: fastest to build, cheapest to extend, and the least likely to age; the brand rests on restraint.
