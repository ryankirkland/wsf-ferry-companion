Honest cost

What this direction demands

  • Six to eight reusable flat shapes to start; new surfaces mostly recombine them, so the asset budget grows slowly.
  • Paper shadows and grain are cheap CSS/SVG tricks, but color discipline is everything; three wrong hues and it turns generic flat-illustration.
  • Cut-paper reads charming at wall size and still crisp at phone size; the shadows need one tuning pass per breakpoint.
  • Payoff: 80% of the storybook's warmth at 40% of its asset budget, with the cleanest scaling story of the illustrated pair.
