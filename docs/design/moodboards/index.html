Three interpretations of illustrated warmth

One family (whimsical, joyful, clean, celebrating Washington State Ferries), three illustration intensities. Every board renders the same live scene and the same three product cards with real vessel names and honest states from the WSF API sample, so only the character differs. Each has a day / dusk / night switch, top right.

How to judge (four tests)

  1. The 9pm wall test. Flip each hero to Night. Would you leave it running on a living room screen?
  2. The phone glance test. Narrow your window. Is the next sailing readable in two seconds?
  3. The celebration test. Does it feel like Washington State Ferries, or like a generic tracker wearing green?
  4. The maintenance test. Read each board's "honest cost" section last, after your heart has picked.

Mix-and-match verdicts are welcome and expected: "Paper Sound water with Evergreen typography" is a perfectly good answer. The winner becomes design tokens, a map visual language, and ADR-0002.
