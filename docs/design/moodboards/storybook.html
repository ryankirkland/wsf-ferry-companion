What this direction demands

  • Roughly 10 to 14 bespoke illustrated assets to start: hull set, two mountain ranges, water bands, clouds, gulls, terminal marks, and every new surface wants one more.
  • Painterly texture and layering need tuning per breakpoint; the wall display and the phone are different paintings.
  • Whimsy needs a disciplined dose. Overused, it turns twee; the guard is restraint in copy and space.
  • Payoff: the strongest 9pm wall presence of the three, and the most distinctive brand.
